**No invitation needed — all users can register and experience it. Registration activation, valid feedback, platform tasks, and inviting friends: four perks remain open.**

This week, MatrixOne Intelligence (hereinafter "MOI") 5.0's **public beta is now live for all users.**

Those who were still waiting for an invitation or haven't joined the Waitlist can now register and get in directly; users who have already activated can simply log in with their existing account — no need to re-register.

From enterprise data preparation and model invocation to agent building, we invite you to bring your real needs and see whether MOI can help you get your hands-on work done.

Meanwhile, there's also a six-hour free event tomorrow: **from 10:24 to 16:24 on November 5, Genesis in MOI offers free access to GLM 5.3 and Kimi K3, with no token usage limits during the event.**

## 01 Public Beta Fully Open — Bring Your Tasks and Try It

If this is your first time with MOI, you can start with the following three types of needs.

**Organize Data and Build a Knowledge Base**

Connect documents, images, audio & video, and business tables to complete parsing, extraction, and processing, turning scattered materials into queryable, maintainable data and knowledge. Documents and emails can also be read in directly, providing a solid basis for subsequent queries and task processing.

**Call Models Through Genesis**

Use the Genesis model token service in MOI to access large models through a unified interface, centrally manage API keys, usage, and costs, and integrate models into your own applications, development tools, and workflows.

**Build Agents with Knowledge and Tools**

Configure enterprise knowledge bases, tools, and skills for agents to support business queries, data analysis, and automated task processing. The knowledge you've already organized and the operators and workflows you've configured can be reused in later tasks.

You can start with a document to process, a question to investigate, or a repetitive task to run — grounding the experience in concrete work.

## 03 Four Perks Continue — Supporting You to Go One Step Further

With the public beta fully open, the following four experience perks remain available.

**Register & Activate: Get 1,000 Credits Instantly**

Register and activate your account for the first time to receive **1,000 Credits**, equivalent to **RMB 100**.

**Submit Valid Feedback: Earn 200 Credits**

Submit issues through the "Feedback" entry on the right side of the page; **once validated, you'll receive 200 Credits**.

**Complete Platform Tasks: Earn Up to 1,500 Credits**

Complete designated platform tasks to earn up to **1,500 Credits** in total — collect experience rewards while exploring the platform's capabilities.

**Invite Friends: Both Sides Get 500 Credits**

Once a friend registers and activates through your invitation link, **both you and your friend receive 500 Credits each — up to 3 friends invited**.

Experience credits previously claimed remain in your original account and will not be cleared by this public beta. All platform billing items are priced in Credits; specific prices can be found under "Product Pricing" in the Billing Center.
